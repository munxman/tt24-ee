Does your company have a specific occupational health question?

A 30-minute Valvekliinik consultation covers one company-specific occupational health question. You receive a short written action plan after the call. Price €99.

Examples of suitable questions

A specific question, a specific answer

  1. 01An employee started work six months ago and the initial health check has not been done. How do we resolve this without it counting as a violation?
  2. 02We have 12 employees and need to carry out a risk assessment. Can we do it ourselves or do we need a service provider? What is the minimum required content?
  3. 03The Labour Inspectorate's (Tööinspektsioon) enforcement campaign covers our sector. Which documents must be in order before an inspection visit?
  4. 04Two employees are refusing to attend occupational health checks. Can we require them to attend? What are the consequences for both parties?
  5. 05Some of our employees work remotely. Does the risk assessment need to cover home offices as well? How is that done in practice?

The consultation does not cover employment contracts, payroll, dismissal or general employment-law disputes. Please consult an employment lawyer for those matters.

Frequently asked questions

About this service

Who is this service for?
Employers operating in Estonia who need a precise answer to a specific occupational health question. Particularly suited to businesses with 5-50 employees whose situation does not fit neatly into general guidance.
What happens after I submit the form?
Valvekliinik contacts you to confirm that the question is suitable and to arrange the consultation.
How much does the consultation cost?
The consultation costs €99. If the company then purchases occupational health services from Valvekliinik, the fee is credited against the first service invoice.
What is not covered?
Employment contracts, payroll, dismissal and general employment-law disputes are outside the scope. Please consult an employment lawyer.
Who provides the consultation?
The consultation is provided or approved by a Valvekliinik occupational health professional.
Is this legal advice?
No. This is practical occupational health guidance. Please consult a lawyer for legal advice in a specific dispute.
